## 中文技术社区短文

Cocolian Office 是一个面向中文办公文档的开源项目。我们希望构建国产化的 LibreOffice，建设可自主构建、可私有部署的云文档中台，让中文文档在浏览器编辑、预览和 PDF 转换时，获得与 Microsoft Office Word 尽可能一致的渲染效果。

项目基于 LibreOffice 和 Collabora Online，持续改进中文字体、段落行距、文档网格、表格跨页和中英文混排。当前主要使用 Mac 版 Word 的 PDF 输出进行对照，结合合成诊断与回归测试验证修改。复杂文档仍可能存在差异，欢迎提供可公开的最小复现案例。

本项目可按各组件许可证免费使用、部署和接入。欢迎文档管理系统、知识库和业务平台通过 WOPI 或转换接口接入；也欢迎贡献代码、测试和文档，或捐款支持持续开发。

项目入口：https://github.com/cocolian/online.mirror

## LinkedIn English post

We are building Cocolian Office, an open source cloud document platform based on LibreOffice and Collabora Online.

Our focus is Chinese document rendering: fonts, line spacing, page grids, mixed Chinese and Latin text, tables and pagination. We compare output against Microsoft Word and use regression tests to guide improvements. Complex documents can still differ, and compatibility work continues.

The platform supports integration through WOPI for document editing and saving, alongside DOCX to PDF conversion. It can be built from source and deployed in your own environment, free of charge under the applicable component licenses.

We welcome integrations, engineering contributions, test cases and donations to support ongoing development.

Project: https://github.com/cocolian/online.mirror
